Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy explains how FlickAtlas ("we", "us") handles information when you visit this website. By using FlickAtlas you agree to the practices described below.

Information we collect

FlickAtlas is a read-only information website. We do not require accounts, and we do not ask you to submit personal information to browse the site. Like most websites, our hosting and analytics providers automatically receive standard technical data when your browser loads a page, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, referring page and the pages you viewed. If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message, which we use only to respond.

Cookies

We and our partners may use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working, understand which pages are popular, and — where advertising is shown — to measure and personalise ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the core content of FlickAtlas remains available without them.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to count visits and understand general audience behaviour in aggregate (for example, which industries and titles are most read). These statistics are not used to identify individual visitors.

Advertising

FlickAtlas may display advertisements served by third-party advertising networks. Those vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites, and each network offers its own controls to opt out of personalised advertising. Advertising partners never receive your name or email address from us.

Third-party services and links

Our pages load film data, images and fonts from external service providers. Those services operate under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external websites.

Data retention and security

Technical logs are retained only as long as needed for security, troubleshooting and aggregate statistics. Email correspondence is kept only as long as necessary to handle your enquiry. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the limited data we hold.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you (for example, emails you sent us). Contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

FlickAtlas is a general-audience information site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted on this page with its revision date.
