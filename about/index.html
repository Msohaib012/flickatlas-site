About FlickAtlas

FlickAtlas is a global entertainment information guide. We bring together the facts film and TV fans actually look for — full cast and crew lists, story summaries, production budgets, worldwide box office earnings, release dates and complete celebrity filmographies — and present them in one clean, organised place, without the clutter.

What we cover

  • Movies & TV shows from every major industry: Bollywood, World Cinema and Lollywood (Pakistani cinema and drama), plus popular titles from around the world.
  • Box office charts — the top grossing films of the year and of all time, with reported budgets shown next to worldwide collections. Where figures allow, we add a clearly labelled verdict estimate (Hit, Average or Flop).
  • Upcoming releases — a month-by-month calendar of movies and TV premieres so you never miss a launch date.
  • Celebrities — biographies, photos, known-for titles and year-by-year filmography tables for actors and creators.

How we work

Every page on FlickAtlas is generated from structured film data and refreshed regularly, so cast lists, ratings and earnings stay current. Box office numbers are shown only where they are genuinely reported — if a budget was never published, our pages say so instead of guessing. Verdicts are estimates based on reported figures and are always labelled as such.

What FlickAtlas is not

FlickAtlas is an information website only. We do not host, stream or link to pirated video, and we do not offer downloads. Trailers on our pages link out to their official sources.
