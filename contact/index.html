✉ Email Us

What to write to us about

  • Corrections — a wrong release date, budget figure, cast member or biography detail. Please include the page link and, if possible, where the correct information can be checked.
  • Suggestions — a movie, show or celebrity you would like to see covered.
  • Advertising & partnerships — business enquiries related to FlickAtlas.
  • Copyright — if you are a rights holder and believe material on this site infringes your rights, email us with the page URL and proof of ownership and we will act promptly.

Response time

We aim to reply within 2–3 working days. FlickAtlas is run by a small team, so during busy release weeks it may take a little longer — corrections are always prioritised.

Please note: FlickAtlas is an information website. We do not stream or sell movies, and we cannot help with subscriptions, tickets or downloads for other services.
