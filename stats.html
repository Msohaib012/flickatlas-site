How to read this: every number counts unique visitors — the same person is counted once per day, and once across the whole 7/30-day window. The ▲▼ chips compare with the previous period (today vs yesterday, 7 days vs the 7 before, and so on). Inside each box: New = first time ever, Returning = seen on an earlier day, Repeat = visited on 2+ different days, Total = daily counts added up. The lists count one view per visitor per page per day, capped at 15 rows each, and started collecting on 1 Nov 2026. "Google visitors landed on" shows the pages people reached from Google — the closest sign of what they searched there; "Searched on FlickAtlas" is what people type in this site's own search box. Days roll over at midnight Pakistan time. This page refreshes itself every 30 seconds.
